About CivicsID

Who runs it

CivicsID is a service of BarNone, Inc., an independent nonprofit. It is not a government website. It is not affiliated with any government agency, and it does not speak for any public office. It doesn't take sides on candidates, parties, bills or ballot measures.

Where the meetings come from

Every listing comes from a public body's own published calendar: city and county meeting portals, school district and library board pages, and the Idaho Legislature's committee agendas. Each night, CivicsID reads those calendars and updates the site. Every meeting links back to the host's own page.

Some public bodies publish a regular schedule ("the first and third Monday at 5 p.m.") instead of a calendar. Those meetings are marked as coming from the host's regular schedule, because they can be cancelled or moved without the change reaching us.

How current it is

Listings refresh every night. Always confirm with the host before you go. Agendas, times and rooms change, sometimes the same day.

The guides are written in plain language and list their sources so you can check them yourself. They are general information, not legal advice.

Found a mistake or a missing meeting?

Report a problem or a missing meeting

Email admin@barnoneinc.org. Tell us what's wrong and where, and include a link to the right information if you have one.

Privacy

CivicsID has no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no tracking. The county and city you pick are saved only in your own browser. Read the privacy page.

Accessibility

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. Pages work with a keyboard and screen readers, text meets contrast standards, and the site works on phones. If something gets in your way, please tell us and we'll fix it.

CivicsID is free to use and asks nothing of you.
