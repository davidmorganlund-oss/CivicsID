Privacy

The short version

CivicsID has no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no tracking or analytics. We don't collect your name, email or address.

What stays on your device

  • Your county and city. When you pick them, your browser remembers them so the calendar opens there next time. That choice is stored only in your browser. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • The app. If you add CivicsID to your home screen, your device keeps a copy of the pages and the latest calendar so it works without a signal.

What leaves your device

  • Address lookups. When you look up your representatives, your browser sends the address straight to the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoder to find your districts. It doesn't pass through us, and we never see or store it.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. We don't get or use those logs.
  • Links you follow. Meeting details, agendas and guide sources open on government and other websites, which have their own privacy policies.
  • Problem reports. If you email us a correction, it goes to BarNone's inbox (admin@barnoneinc.org). We use it only to fix the site.

Updated November 2026. Questions: admin@barnoneinc.org.
