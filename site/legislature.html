How to testify

  1. Find the bill on a committee's agenda below. Agendas usually post a few days ahead.
  2. Open the committee's page and use its Testimony Registration link to sign up to speak in person or remotely, or to submit written testimony.
  3. Keep it short: who you are, where you live, and how the bill would affect you. Committees often limit each person to a few minutes.
