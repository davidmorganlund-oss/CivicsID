Find who represents you.

Enter an Idaho street address to see your state legislators and members of Congress and how to reach them, plus where to find your city and county officials.

Your address is sent from your browser to the U.S. Census Bureau's address lookup to find your districts. CivicsID never receives or stores it. Your browser remembers only your county and city, so the Engage guides can show local links.

Your representatives will show up here.

Not sure what to say once you have a name? Read how to contact the people who represent you.

State legislators come from Open States, a public roster built from the Idaho Legislature's member pages, and members of Congress from a public roster. Both are refreshed nightly. County links cover all 44 counties where we could confirm a website, and city links cover some of Idaho's larger cities.
